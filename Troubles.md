# UCR Housing Conduct Policies — Possible Trouble
*Quick reference based on the 2025-2026 Housing Resident Conduct Policies*

##  Serious/High-Stakes Violations
- **Weapons**: Firearms, ammo, explosives, knives with blades over 2½" (kitchen knives for cooking OK), slingshots, martial arts weapons — even replicas/facsimiles are banned unless pre-approved for specific academic/activity use.
- **Drugs**: Possessing, using, selling, or having paraphernalia for illegal drugs is strictly prohibited — medical marijuana cards aren't recognized by the university.
- **Violence/Threats/Harassment**: Includes stalking, bullying, intimidation, unwanted contact — physical, verbal, written, or electronic.
- **Sexual misconduct**: Any form (harassment, assault, non-consent, etc.) — handled with extra seriousness and may involve Title IX.
- **Fire/Arson**: Causing or contributing to a fire, even unintentionally.
- **Hazing**: Any initiation activity causing harm or degradation.
- **Theft or vandalism** of university or others' property.

##  Alcohol
- **Residence Halls**: Zero tolerance — no alcohol at all, any age, opened or unopened, even just being in its presence.
- **Campus Apartments**: OK only if everyone present is 21+; can't be left unattended where minors have access.
- Drinking games (beer pong, flip cup, etc.) and related paraphernalia (kegs, funnels, beer bongs) are banned everywhere.
- Empty alcohol containers/displays not allowed in halls or in shared spaces with under-21 residents.
- No alcohol in public spaces (balconies, lounges, hallways, pools) regardless of age.

##  Smoking/Vaping
- Completely banned — smoking, vaping, tobacco, hookahs, bongs, glass pipes — indoors *and* on adjacent outdoor property.

##  Guests
- **Guest limit**: 6 guests max per assigned space at a time.
- **Overnight guests**: Need roommate approval. Halls = 1/night per resident; Apartments = 2/bedroom/night. Max 3 days/2 nights per guest, per month.
- Can't leave guests unattended.
- You're responsible for your guests' behavior — their violations can become your violations.
- Don't let in people on the exclusionary list or unescorted strangers.

##  Noise / Quiet Hours
- **Regular Quiet Hours**: 10 PM–8 AM weeknights; 1 AM–10 AM weekends.
- **Finals Week**: 24-hour quiet hours starting 5 PM on the last day of instruction.
- Amplified sound (speakers in windows/doors) banned at all times.

##  Room/Facility Stuff
- **Decorations**: Max 25% of any surface; no wallpaper, paint, holes, or hanging hooks on walls/ceilings. Only fireproof holiday decorations, no live cut trees.
- **Furniture**: Can't loft beds without approval; can't remove common-area furniture; personal furniture must be fire-rated.
- **Doors/locks**: No tampering, no extra locks, no propping doors open, no lending your key/card or door code.
- **Windows**: Can't cover (except approved coverings), can't use as entry/exit, can't hang items out of them, screens can't be removed.
- **Pets**: Only fish (20 gal max halls / 40 gal max apartments) unless it's a registered service/ESA animal pre-approved by Housing. Unauthorized pets = possible eviction + fumigation fees.
- **Cooking**: Open-element appliances (hot plates, toasters, etc.) banned in bedrooms/hall rooms — kitchen only. Must attend all cooking. Setting off fire alarms via burnt food/incense/vaping counts as a violation.
- **Candles**: Banned unless wicks are removed/clipped.
- No BBQs/grills/smokers anywhere on property.

##  Vehicles & Micromobility
- Bikes/scooters/skateboards must be registered (TAPS) and can't be ridden indoors.
- No trick riding/stunts.
- Vehicles only in designated parking; no washing or major repairs on-site.

##  Computing
- No installing software/hardware on lab computers.
- No file sharing/pirating copyrighted material (DMCA).
- No routers/hubs on university-issued internet, no static IPs, no hosting servers.
- No sharing passwords or masking your identity online.

##  Dining Halls
- Must show your own ID (non-transferable, can't loan your meal plan).
- Can't take food out except one piece of fruit/dessert.
- No re-entry once you leave.
- Dress code required (shirt, shoes, etc.)

##  Fire/Safety Basics
- Never disable/tamper with smoke detectors, extinguishers, or sprinklers.
- Don't block doorways/exits.
- Evacuate immediately when alarms go off; don't re-enter until cleared.

##  Other Bans
- Gambling for money/value (games for fun only are fine).
- Soliciting/selling without authorization.
- Water fights/balloons/water guns anywhere on property.
- Drones and running indoors.
- Throwing/dropping anything from windows, balconies, or stairwells.
- Sidewalk chalk writing.
- Subleasing your space.
- Recording/broadcasting other residents without their permission.

##  If Something Happens
- You'll typically be contacted within **3 business days** of a report, and meet within **5 working days**.
- **Administrative Meeting** = informal, no responsibility decided.
- **Administrative Review Meeting** = formal, can result in a responsibility finding + sanctions.
- Skipping meetings can trigger a decision without your input *and* an academic hold on your account.
- **Appeals**: Must file in writing within **3 business days** of the decision letter, and only for: (1) excessive sanction, (2) new evidence, or (3) denied due process.
- Parents *can* be notified if you're found responsible for underage drinking or drug possession.
