# UCR Housing Conduct Policies — Possible Troubles

##  Serious/High-Stakes Violations

**Weapons** — *Policy 1.34*
> "Possession of firearms, ammunition, explosives, chemical weapons, other dangerous weapons, and replicas of dangerous weapons are prohibited in all Housing properties and their adjacent properties."
Knife limit: *"knives with blades longer than 2 ½ inches (with the exception of kitchen knives used in food preparation)"* are banned as weapons.

**Drugs** — *Policy 1.07.1*
> "Delivering, furnishing, transferring, manufacturing, using, and possessing illicit drugs or drug paraphernalia is strictly prohibited at the university. Additionally, the university does not recognize the privileges associated with a medical marijuana card."

**Violence/Threats/Harassment** — *Policy 1.01.2*
> "Direct and indirect forms of abuse, threats, coercion, harassment, intimidation, stalking, bullying, unwanted personal contact, violence against another person or their property or causing the reasonable apprehension of physical or verbal harm, are prohibited."

**Sexual Misconduct** — *Policy 1.22*
> "Sexual offenses of any nature, including but not limited to sexual harassment, sexual violence, intimate partner violence, dating violence, sexual assault, non-consent, incapacitation, and stalking, are prohibited."

**Fire/Arson** — *Policy 1.12.2*
> "Causing, attempting to cause, or contributing to the causation or continuation of a fire, either intentionally or unintentionally, within UCR Housing facilities or on their adjacent properties, is prohibited."

**Hazing** — *Policy 1.15*
> "Hazing includes any method of initiation or pre-initiation into a campus organization or other activity engaged in by the organization or members of the organization at any time that causes, or is likely to cause, physical injury, personal degradation, disgrace, or psychological harm to any student or other person."

**Theft/Vandalism** — *Policy 1.29.1 & 1.33*
> "Theft of, conversion of, destruction of, or damage to any property of the University, or any property of others while on University premises... is prohibited." / "Vandalism, unintentional damage, or destruction of University or personal property is not permitted."

##  Alcohol

**Residence Halls** — *Policy 1.02.5*
> "Alcohol consumption, possession, manufacture, or distribution in any form, or being in the presence of alcohol in any form, is not permitted in the Residence Halls or the areas immediately adjacent to them regardless of resident's or guest's age."

**Campus Apartments** — *Policy 1.02.1*
> "Alcohol in any form may not be distributed to and/or consumed by, or in the presence of, persons under the age of 21... Alcohol may not be left unattended in the common area of an apartment or in any space shared with residents under the age of 21."

**Drinking games** — *Policy 1.02.1*
> "Drinking games or simulated drinking games are prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to beer pong, water pong, (or another beverage), flip cups, quarters, and other variants."

**Empty containers** — *Policy 1.02.3*
> "Empty or Repurposed alcohol containers, collections, and/or displays... are not permitted within or on grounds immediately adjacent to Residence Hall facilities or in spaces within the Campus Apartments shared with one or more residents under the age of 21."

**Public spaces** — *Policy 1.02.4*
> "Alcohol in any form may not be consumed, possessed, manufactured or distributed in public spaces (including but not limited to: balconies, patios, lobbies, hallways, lounges, meeting rooms, public restrooms, suites, and swimming pools)."

##  Smoking/Vaping — *Policy 1.23*
> "The University prohibits smoking and the use of tobacco products in all interior, exterior, and parking areas of University-controlled properties... smoking, the use of smokeless tobacco products (such as dip and chew), vaping, and the use of unregulated nicotine products (such as electronic cigarettes) are strictly prohibited."

##  Guests

**Guest limit** — *Policy 1.14.3*
> "There is a limit of 6 guests, in addition to the residents of an assigned space, allowed in an assigned campus apartment or residence hall room."

**Overnight guests** — *Policy 1.14.4*
> "Having overnight guest(s) without the approval of your roommate(s) is prohibited. Each resident in the Residence Halls is limited to one overnight guest per night. In Campus Apartments, there is a limit of two overnight guests per bedroom per night... There is a three-day, two-night limit for each guest, per month."

**Unattended guests** — *Policy 1.14.5*
> "Residents are not permitted to leave guests unattended in residential areas."

**Responsibility for guests** — *Policy 1.14.2*
> "Residents are responsible for, and may be held accountable for, the actions of guests at all times within UCR Housing facilities and on their adjacent properties. Guests must follow all UCR Housing Residential Conduct Policies."

##  Noise / Quiet Hours

**Regular Quiet Hours** — *Policy 1.28.4*
> "Sunday, 10 pm - Monday, 8 am / Monday, 10 pm -Tuesday, 8 am / Tuesday, 10 pm - Wednesday, 8 am / Wednesday, 10 pm - Thursday, 8 am / Thursday, 10 pm - Friday, 8 am / Saturday, 1am - Saturday, 10 am / Sunday, 1 am - Sunday, 10 am"

**Finals Week** — *Policy 1.28.3*
> "Beginning at 5 pm on the final day of instruction each quarter, 24-hour Quiet Hours are in effect for the remainder of the academic quarter."

**Amplified sound** — *Policy 1.28.1*
> "Amplified sound is not permitted within UCR Housing facilities or on their adjacent properties. Residents are not to place audio speakers in doorways or windows or otherwise amplify sound from doorways or windows to the outside."

##  Room/Facility Stuff

**Decorations** — *Policy 2.11.2 & 2.11.3*
> "Decorations may cover no more than 25% of any surface." / "Residents are not permitted to wallpaper, paint, puncture, sticker, otherwise alter or hang anything from unit ceilings or walls."

**Beds/lofting** — *Policy 2.16.1*
> "Alterations to beds, such as lofting, is prohibited... Waterbeds are not permitted in UCR housing facilities."

**Doors/locks** — *Policy 2.12.1*
> "Altering or tampering with doors, doorjambs, door closures, doorknobs, and locking mechanisms is prohibited. Installation of any additional door locking mechanism (e.g., auto closure, deadbolt, latch, chain, etc.) is prohibited."

**Key/card sharing** — *Policy 2.17.1*
> "Keys/cards are not to be duplicated, loaned, or furnished to any other person. Additionally, providing any other person a lock access code is prohibited."

**Windows** — *Policy 2.31.1*
> "Windows are not to be covered or visually obstructed, with the exception of UCR residential education and housing approved postings and/or university-issued window coverings, this includes a prohibition on the use of flags, boards, foil, and paper to cover the window."

**Pets** — *Policy 1.18*
> "With exception of Housing approved, registered Service Animals and or Emotional Support Animals residents are not permitted to keep pets, except for fish in an aquarium, specifically for Residence Halls 20-gallon maximum per room and for Campus Apartments 40-gallon maximum per apartment... Keeping an [unauthorized] pet is grounds for immediate eviction."

**Cooking appliances** — *Policy 2.15.1*
> "Open element appliances such as, but not limited to, hot plates, indoor grills, immersion heating coils, toasters, toaster ovens, soldering irons, and heat guns are prohibited in campus apartment bedrooms and all residence hall spaces with the exception of kitchens."

**Candles** — *Policy 2.15.2*
> "Possession, storage, or use of flammable substances including gasoline, propane, butane, kerosene, food warming fuels, candles (except with clipped or removed wicks)... is prohibited within UCR housing facilities."

**BBQs/grills** — *Policy 2.03*
> "Personal barbecues, smokers, and grills are not permitted within UCR housing facilities or on their adjacent properties."

##  Vehicles & Micromobility

**Registration** — *Policy 2.06.2*
> "All bicycles, mopeds under 50cc, and personal micromobility devices are required to be registered with Transportation and Parking Services (TAPS) and must display the issued compliance sticker."

**Indoor use** — *Policy 2.06.1*
> "Bicycles, mopeds, skateboards, roller blades, roller skates, scooters, and other similar devices, including approved battery-powered personal micromobility devices, are not to be used inside any housing facilities."

**Trick riding** — *Policy 2.06.3*
> "Trick riding and stunts are prohibited within UCR housing facilities and on their adjacent properties."

##  Computing

**Lab installs** — *Policy 4.01.3*
> "Installation of any hardware (except flash drives) or software on the lab computers is prohibited."

**Copyright/piracy** — *Policy 4.02.4*
> "Unauthorized distribution and/or reproduction of any U.S. copyright law protected material, including but not limited to software, video, digital books, and/or audio files, is prohibited."

**Routers** — *Policy 4.03.4*
> "Use of routers, bridges, switches, access points, and/or hubs is not permitted in areas where University-issued internet access is provided."

**Password sharing** — *Policy 4.02.1*
> "Residents are prohibited from intentionally or unintentionally furnishing their account passwords to others for any Housing computing system."

##  Dining Halls

**ID/card use** — *Policy 3.01.2*
> "This card is not transferable and no one else is allowed to use your card. Dining plan holders may not pass, loan, or sell meals or their ID card to anyone for any reason."

**Taking food out** — *Policy 3.01.8*
> "No food may be taken out of a residential restaurant with the exception of one piece of fruit or one dessert item."

**Re-entry** — *Policy 3.01.7*
> "Individuals including guests are not permitted to enter a residential restaurant without purchase. Leaving and re-entry into a residential restaurant is not permitted."

**Dress code** — *Policy 3.01.6*
> "In accordance with the State Health Code, residents and guests must be fully dressed which includes upper and lower body coverings and footwear."

##  Fire/Safety Basics

**Fire safety equipment** — *Policy 1.12.3*
> "Tampering with, relocating, disabling, or disarming fire safety equipment and/or CO2 detectors or using any such equipment for any purpose other than for the prevention or control of fire is not permitted."

**Blocking exits** — *Policy 1.12.4*
> "Blocking or obstructing doorways, windows, or pathways used to enter and exit any UCR Housing Facilities areas is prohibited."

**Evacuation** — *Policy 1.09*
> "All residents and guests are required to leave the building in a reasonable and timely manner whenever the fire alarm activates or when directed to do so by UCR staff or emergency personnel... Residents are not permitted to re-enter an evacuated building until authorized by UCR staff or emergency personnel."

##  Other Bans

**Gambling** — *Policy 1.13*
> "Gambling within UCR Housing facilities or on their adjacent properties is prohibited. This includes, but is not limited to, activities played for money, checks, credit, or any other item representative of value... Games of chance played solely for recreation, but not for exchange of goods, services, or actions, are allowed."

**Soliciting** — *Policy 1.24*
> "No advertising, recruiting, campaigning, selling, or soliciting is permitted within UCR Housing facilities or on their adjacent properties without proper authorization and in accordance with soliciting guidelines."

**Water games** — *Policy 1.25.2*
> "Water fights, water guns, water balloons, water pong, or other games involving water/liquid are prohibited within UCR Housing facilities and on their adjacent properties."

**Drones/running** — *Policy 1.25.1*
> "Running and usage of aerial devices (such as drones) are specifically prohibited within residential facilities."

**Throwing objects** — *Policy 1.30*
> "Throwing, dropping, projecting, or allowing any object or material to fall from any residential community window, balcony, railing, bridge, or stairwell is prohibited."

**Sidewalk chalk** — *Policy 2.27*
> "Writing on sidewalks is prohibited."

**Subleasing** — *Policy 2.28*
> "Subleasing of housing spaces is prohibited, including any kind of short or long term rental for money or any other item representative of value."

**Recording others** — *Policy 1.08.3*
> "Residents may not broadcast or record the images or voices of other residents or guests in any residential space, common area, and/or adjacent property without their expressed knowledge and permission."

##  If Got Into Trouble

**Timeline** — *FAQ, p.5*
> "From the time a report is filed, the RD/AD will typically contact you within 3 business days... After this contact is made, you can expect to have a meeting, most often with the RD/AD, within 5 working days."

**Administrative Meeting** — *FAQ, p.5*
> "In an Administrative meeting, the RD/AD will not assess responsibility for policy violations. The RD/AD and the resident will consider potential follow-up options."

**Administrative Review Meeting** — *FAQ, p.5*
> "Based upon your input and all of the information, including from other involved individuals, the RD/AD will decide to what extent you are responsible for the policy violation(s)."

**Missing a meeting** — *FAQ, p.5–6*
> "Should you take no action to schedule or attend an Administrative Review Meeting, a decision may be made without the benefit of your input... Additionally, failing to schedule or attend a meeting may result in an academic hold being placed on your account."

**Appeals** — *FAQ, p.6–7*
> "An appeal must be made in writing, using the link provided in the decision letter sent by your hearing officer, within three business days of the decision."
Valid grounds: *"Severity of the sanction is inappropriate based on what you were found responsible for / Introduction of new evidence not available at the hearing / Lack of procedural due process."*

**Parental notification** — *FAQ, p.6*
> "Parents/Guardians can be notified when a student is found responsible for an underage drinking violation or any illegal drug possession violation."
